# Type 2 Diabetes Management Protocol (Synthetic)

> SYNTHETIC EDUCATIONAL CONTENT. Not medical guidance. Numbers are illustrative
> and simplified for a software demo. Do not use for real patient care.

## Glycemic targets
- A common HbA1c target is < 7.0% for many non-frail adults.
- For older or frail adults, a relaxed target (e.g., < 8.0%) may be appropriate
  to reduce hypoglycemia risk. Targets are individualized by a clinician.

## Titration guidance (clinician-directed)
- Metformin is typical first-line therapy when eGFR allows
  (avoid initiation if eGFR < 30; review dosing if eGFR < 45).
- If HbA1c is above target on metformin, additional agents (e.g., SGLT2 inhibitor,
  GLP-1 receptor agonist) may be added by a clinician based on comorbidities such
  as cardiovascular or kidney disease.
- Insulin initiation is a clinician decision and generally warrants specialist or
  primary-care review; an automated tool must NOT recommend starting or dosing
  insulin on its own.

## Escalation / urgent review criteria
- Symptoms of severe hyperglycemia (marked thirst, confusion, very high glucose)
  or suspected DKA -> urgent escalation.
- Recurrent hypoglycemia -> clinician review to adjust therapy.

## Monitoring
- Recheck HbA1c approximately every 3 months while adjusting therapy, then every
  6 months once stable.
