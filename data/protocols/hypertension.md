# Hypertension Management Protocol (Synthetic)

> SYNTHETIC EDUCATIONAL CONTENT. Not medical guidance. Numbers are illustrative
> and simplified for a software demo. Do not use for real patient care.

## Blood pressure targets
- General adult target: < 130/80 mmHg when tolerated.
- Adults >= 75 years or frail: an individualized target up to < 140/90 mmHg
  may be appropriate to limit orthostatic risk.

## Escalation / urgent review criteria
- Systolic BP (SBP) >= 180 mmHg or diastolic BP (DBP) >= 110 mmHg on a confirmed
  reading is a hypertensive crisis range and requires SAME-DAY clinician review.
- New symptoms (chest pain, shortness of breath, vision change, focal weakness)
  with elevated BP require urgent escalation regardless of the numeric value.

## Titration guidance (clinician-directed)
- If BP is above target on a stable regimen across two or more readings, options
  include up-titrating the current agent or adding a second first-line agent.
- First-line agents: ACE inhibitor (e.g., lisinopril), ARB, thiazide diuretic,
  or dihydropyridine calcium-channel blocker (e.g., amlodipine).
- Before increasing an ACE inhibitor or ARB, check that potassium is within range
  and eGFR is stable. Hold up-titration if potassium > 5.0 mmol/L.
- Do NOT combine an ACE inhibitor and an ARB.

## Monitoring
- After any medication change, recheck BP in 2-4 weeks and recheck potassium and
  renal function within 1-2 weeks for renin-angiotensin agents.
